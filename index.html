Daily log

One log per service. The report writes itself.

Head chefs fill this in at the end of service — roughly nine fields, mostly taps. Saving it creates a dated report you can reopen, review and correct before it goes anywhere.

How it flows

  • The head chef saves the day’s log. It appears under Daily summary report as a dated entry they can reopen and edit.
  • Each chef sees only their own kitchen. The Executive Chef sees both.
  • Only the Executive Chef can release a report to the Management dashboard. Once released, it locks for the head chef.
  • Exceptions — failed checks, deviations, faults, complaints — become action-plan rows on their own, with a named owner and a deadline.
